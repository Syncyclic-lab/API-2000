Accessibility statement

Prepared 6 October 2026

We want the API 2000 Venting Calculator to be usable by everyone, including people who use a keyboard, screen reader, magnification or high-contrast settings.

Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the level referenced by EN 301 549 in Europe and generally applied under the Americans with Disabilities Act and Canadian accessibility laws.

Conformance status

Partially conformant. Most of the site meets WCAG 2.1 AA; the known exceptions are listed below.

What we have done

  • Every form field has a visible label that is linked to it, and hints are linked as descriptions.
  • Text and form-control borders meet the WCAG contrast ratios in light and dark themes.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a “skip to calculator” link.
  • The input tabs follow the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern, and pass/fail results are shown in words, not only colour.
  • When a calculation finishes, the outcome is announced to screen readers and focus moves to the results.
  • The layout reflows at 320 px width and at 200–400 % zoom; motion is reduced if you ask your system for it.

Known limitations

  • Wide results tables scroll horizontally on small screens.
  • Engineering notation (symbols such as ΔP, Rᵢ and section references) may be read inconsistently by some screen readers.
  • The calculator needs JavaScript. The legal and information pages do not.
  • We have tested with automated checks, keyboard-only use and browser accessibility tools. We have not yet completed testing with every major screen reader.

Feedback

If you meet a barrier or need results in another format, contact us at [contact email address]. We aim to reply within 10 working days.

How we assessed this site

Self-evaluation by the operator, using automated contrast and markup checks, keyboard testing and manual review against WCAG 2.1 AA, on 6 October 2026.
