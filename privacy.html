Privacy notice

Effective 6 October 2026

In short: the calculator runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, receive or store the values you enter. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party fonts or scripts. Our hosting provider, GitHub, logs technical connection data such as your IP address.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for this website is the operator named in the legal notice: [Operator legal name], [postal address], [contact email address]. Use the same address for any privacy question or request.

2. Data you enter into the calculator

Everything you type, including the optional tag number, project name and “prepared by” fields, is processed only in your browser’s memory to compute the results. It is never sent to us or to anyone else and is not saved: it is gone when you reload or close the page. “Print / save PDF” uses your browser’s own print function on your device.

3. Hosting and server logs (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When your browser loads a page, it necessarily sends technical data to GitHub’s servers: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser’s user agent. According to GitHub’s documentation, visitor IP addresses of GitHub Pages sites are logged and stored for security purposes. We do not receive these logs.

Our legal basis for using a hosting provider is our legitimate interest in making the calculator available reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / UK GDPR). GitHub processes this data under its own privacy statement, which also explains how it protects data transferred outside the EU/EEA and the UK and how long it keeps logs.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not set cookies and does not read or write browser storage (local storage, session storage or IndexedDB). It loads no resources from third parties: fonts, styles, images and scripts are all served from this site. Because nothing is stored on your device, no cookie consent is requested.

5. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioural advertising (California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and similar US state laws).
  • We do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children; the site is a professional engineering tool.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data from the calculator, most requests will concern emails you sent us. Contact us at the address above; we respond within the time limits that apply to you (for example one month under the GDPR, 45 days under the CCPA).

  • EU/EEA: you may complain to the data protection supervisory authority in your country of residence or work, or where an alleged infringement occurred.
  • United Kingdom: you may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
  • Canada: you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, in Quebec, to the Commission d’accès à l’information.
  • United States: residents of states with privacy laws (such as California) may exercise the rights those laws give them by contacting us; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

If the way this site handles data changes, for example if analytics were ever added, we will update this notice and its effective date before the change takes effect, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
